⚡ ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

Making EV Charging Simple & Smarter

EV Charge Finder is a web-based platform that helps electric vehicle users find nearby charging stations, check availability and book charging slots easily.

🚗

What is EV Charge Finder?

EV Charge Finder is designed to make electric vehicle charging more convenient. Users can search for charging stations based on their location, view station details, check available charging slots and make bookings online.

The system also provides separate dashboards for users, station owners and administrators so that each type of user can manage their activities efficiently.

🎯

Our Purpose

The main purpose of this system is to reduce the time required to find an available EV charging station and provide a simple online booking experience.

It helps EV users plan their charging journey before reaching the station.
